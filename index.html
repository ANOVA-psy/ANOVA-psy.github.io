我研究的是人怎么听懂别人：噪声里怎么抓住一句话、口型和语气能补上多少、又怎么猜到对方在想什么。 I study how people understand each other — how you catch a sentence in noise, how much mouth shape and tone fill in, and how you guess what someone is thinking.

这几年我把同样的问题拿去问大模型：它能理解到什么程度，又在哪儿露馅。研究之外，我把反复出现的麻烦做成了能用的工具，下面两个项目就是这么来的。 Lately I put the same questions to large language models: how far their understanding goes, and where it breaks. Outside the lab I turn recurring friction into working tools — the two projects below came out of that.

视听语音整合训练系统Audio-Visual Integration Training System

Audio-Visual Speech Integration Training

被试在不同噪声下判断听到的音节，答对答错推动难度自动升降，最后用心理物理曲线拟合出阈值，折算成 0–100 分。四类能力、三个阶段，被试点完一次会拿到一张带曲线的报告。 Listeners identify syllables under varying noise; responses move the difficulty up and down, and a psychometric curve turns the result into a threshold and a 0–100 score. Four abilities, three phases, one report with its curves.

中学教师教学评估与改进智能体Teaching Evaluation & Improvement Agent

LLM agent for secondary-school teaching review

把一节课的教学设计和课堂转写交给模型，按四个维度打分，每个分数都要对回原文的某个时间点，再给出可以动手改的建议。我负责把「评课」拆成模型能执行的任务。 Lesson plans and transcripts go in; four dimension scores come out, each pinned to a timestamp in the transcript, followed by changes a teacher can act on. My part was breaking "lesson review" into steps a model can execute.

更多关于我的研究兴趣与技能More on interests and skills

我做的其实是两件事。一件是把模糊的感觉变成能测的数字——「在噪声里听不清」到底是几个 dB。另一件是把这套流程里最烦的部分写成工具，先自己用，用着别扭就改。 Two things, mostly. One is turning a vague impression into a number — "hard to hear in noise" is really a threshold in dB. The other is writing the most annoying part of that workflow into a tool, using it myself first, and fixing whatever feels wrong.
