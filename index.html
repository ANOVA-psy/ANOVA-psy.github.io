Psychology × Cognitive Science × AI

刘慧敏Huimin Liu

心理学硕士在读 · 华南师范大学 M.A. Student in Psychology · South China Normal University

我现在主要研究大模型的心智理论，两个方向：一是能力边界——模型在理解他人的想法、意图和社会情境时，究竟卡在哪一步；二是训练改良——先弄清它为什么卡，再看能不能靠训练把它推过去。 My current work is on theory of mind in large language models, in two directions: where the capability ends — at which step a model stops tracking other people's beliefs, intentions and social situations — and how to improve training from there.

方法还是心理学的老办法：把「读懂别人」拆成能测的任务，用实验拿证据，再回过来解释模型为什么会这样。课题组里的平台和系统，多半是这套流程里绕不开的麻烦长出来的。 The method is the psychologist's method: break "understanding others" into measurable tasks, get evidence from experiments, then explain why the model behaved that way. The platforms below grew out of the friction in that loop.

视听语音整合训练系统Audio-Visual Integration Training System

Audio-Visual Speech Integration Training

课题组设计并主导的实验平台，我负责前端实现：被试在不同噪声下判断听到的音节，答对答错推动难度自动升降，最后拟合出阈值、折算成 0–100 分。 A lab-designed platform; I built the front end. Listeners identify syllables under varying noise, responses move the difficulty up and down, and a psychometric curve turns the result into a threshold and a 0–100 score.

中学教师教学评估与改进智能体Teaching Evaluation & Improvement Agent

LLM agent for secondary-school teaching review

把一节课的教学设计和课堂转写交给模型，按四个维度打分，每个分数都要对回原文的某个时间点，再给出可以动手改的建议。我负责把「评课」拆成模型能执行的任务。 Lesson plans and transcripts go in; four dimension scores come out, each pinned to a timestamp in the transcript, followed by changes a teacher can act on. My part was breaking "lesson review" into steps a model can execute.

更多关于我的研究兴趣与技能More on interests and skills

心理学给我的训练是「把话说清楚」：任何模糊的判断都要落成可测量的任务。「模型不理解人」不算结论，「它在二阶错误信念上掉到随机水平」才算。 Psychology trained me to make claims measurable. "The model doesn't understand people" is not a finding; "it drops to chance on second-order false belief" is.

做东西的习惯来自另一边：拆任务、搭流程、把重复的活写成工具。这两件事在我现在的研究里其实是同一件——先用心智理论的范式把边界测出来，再照着边界去改训练模型的方式。课题组里的平台和系统，是这条路上的副产品。 The habit of building came from elsewhere: splitting tasks, wiring up workflows, writing the repetitive parts into tools. In my current work the two are the same thing — measure the boundary with theory-of-mind paradigms, then use that boundary to change how the model is trained. The lab platforms are a by-product of that loop.
