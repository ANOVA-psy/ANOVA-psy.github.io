研究Research

大模型的心智理论评测Measuring theory of mind in large language models

MIST 将心智理论表现拆为能力、社交性和道德三个维度，并通过词汇联想与情感归因两个间接任务，对 8 个模型进行比较。我的工作包括实验设计、数据分析与结果绘图。 MIST splits theory-of-mind performance into competence, sociability and morality, and compares eight models through two indirect tasks — word association and affective attribution. My part was experiment design, analysis and the figures.
