用心理学提出可检验的问题，用实验和数据分析回答它，再把研究里反复出现的需求做成真正能用的工具。 I frame testable questions from psychology, answer them with experiments and data analysis, then turn the recurring needs inside that work into tools people can actually use.

我在两件事之间来回：一件是把问题问对——用实验设计把"感觉"变成可测量的指标；另一件是把答案用起来——把流程里最麻烦的部分做成工具。做平台和做 Agent 都是后者：先自己在真实场景里用，再按用起来不顺的地方改。 I move back and forth between two things: getting the question right, turning a hunch into something measurable; and putting the answer to work, turning the most painful part of a workflow into a tool. Both projects above are the second kind — used in a real setting first, then rebuilt wherever it felt wrong.
