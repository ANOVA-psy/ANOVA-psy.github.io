关于我About

心理学硕士在读。现在主要研究大模型的心智理论：先摸清它理解他人想法与意图的能力边界，再用心理学的实验范式去找原因，最后回到训练上看能不能改进。 A psychology master's student. My current work is on theory of mind in LLMs: mapping where a model's understanding of beliefs and intentions ends, using psychological paradigms to find out why, and then going back to training to see if it can be improved.
