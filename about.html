关于我About

华南师范大学心理学硕士在读。现在的研究方向是大模型的心智理论评测：先界定模型在理解他人意图与信念时卡在哪一步，再回到实验条件与训练上，看原因和改进空间。 Master's student in psychology at South China Normal University. My current work measures theory of mind in large language models: first locating the step where a model stops tracking beliefs and intentions, then going back to task conditions and training to look for causes and room to improve.
