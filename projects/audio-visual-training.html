为什么做这个Why this exists

日常交流里，我们其实一直在做多感官整合：嘈杂环境下盯着对方口型会更容易听懂；声音和画面差了几十毫秒，理解就会变吃力。这些能力在听损、康复训练和老年人群体中差异很大，但很难用一句"听得清不清楚"描述。 Everyday listening is multisensory: watching a speaker's mouth helps in noise, and a few tens of milliseconds of audio-visual asynchrony makes comprehension harder. These abilities vary widely across listeners — but "can you hear it clearly?" is not a measurable answer.

这个平台要解决的是"把它测出来"：把四种能力拆成四个可独立施测的任务，每个任务用心理物理阶梯逼近阈值，再用统一的 0–100 分体系呈现，让训练前后的变化可以被比较。 This platform makes it measurable: four separable abilities become four tasks, each converging on a threshold through a staircase procedure, all reported on one 0–100 scale so change over training can be compared.

两拨人要用它：被试得一眼看懂现在该做什么；实验者得能改参数、回看历史、导出数据。 Two people have to use it: the participant, who needs to know what to do without being told twice, and the experimenter, who needs to change parameters, review history and export data.

Workflow

三阶段实验流程Three-phase procedure

同一个被试在同一次会话里走完三段，前后测用同一套刺激与拟合方法，差异才可比。One session per participant; pre- and post-test share stimuli and fitting method so the difference is comparable.

01 PRE
前测 · 建立基线Pre-test · baseline
按预设顺序遍历各档 SNR / 模糊度 / SOA，逐试次记录正误，拟合心理物理曲线得到该被试的基线阈值。Walk through preset SNR, blur and SOA levels; fit a psychometric curve per module to get baseline thresholds.
02 TRAIN
训练 · 自适应难度Training · adaptive
读取前测拟合结果推荐每个模块的起始难度，再由阶梯控制器按作答动态升降，跑满试次或反转次数后结束。Starting difficulty is derived from pre-test fits, then a staircase controller moves it up or down by response.
03 POST
后测 · 量化变化Post-test · change
重复前测设计，输出同一套阈值与分数，与前测对比得到训练效应；历史页可跨会话看趋势。Repeat the pre-test design; compare thresholds and scores with baseline, with cross-session trends on the history page.

起点推导：从上一个阈值出发Starting point from the last threshold

AdaptiveEngine

例如功能一取"前测阈值 + 2 dB"作为首个 SNR，使开局正确率接近九成，避免一上来就挫败；视觉整合则按视觉增益分档决定起始模糊度。所有推导值都会被夹到预设合理区间内。Module one starts at "baseline threshold + 2 dB" so the first trials are mostly correct; visual integration picks blur level by visual gain. Every derived value is clamped to a sane range.

阶梯控制：不同任务不同规则Staircase: a different rule per task

StaircaseController

功能一用 2-down/1-up 调 SNR；功能二用 3-down/1-up 推进模糊度档位；功能三按视觉/听觉领先两侧分别外推与内缩时窗边界；功能四在主轴上调 SNR、触及安全上限后改调干扰源衰减。Module 1: 2-down/1-up on SNR. Module 2: 3-down/1-up across blur levels. Module 3: push or pull each side of the temporal window. Module 4: SNR first, then distractor attenuation once it hits the ceiling.

Four Modules

四个训练模块，四种能力Four modules, four abilities

每个模块只量一件事，指标单位统一为 dB 或 ms，便于互相解释。Each module measures one thing, in dB or ms, so results stay interpretable.

功能一 · 噪音抗干扰（听觉基底）Module 1 · Speech in noise (auditory baseline)

threshold · dB

在不同信噪比下识别语音，拟合出正确率达到判准时的 SNR 阈值——越低说明越能在噪声里听清。Identify speech at varying SNR; the fitted threshold is the SNR at criterion — lower is better.

功能二 · 视觉整合（看口型代偿）Module 2 · Visual compensation (speechreading)

gain · dB

固定听觉条件，比较"有清晰口型"与"口型模糊"两种条件下的阈值差，差值即视觉增益。Holding audio conditions fixed, compare thresholds with clear versus blurred mouth cues; the difference is visual gain.

功能三 · 异步校准（时空整合）Module 3 · Temporal recalibration

window · ms

让声音与画面在时间轴上错开（视觉领先或听觉领先），测出仍能整合成单一事件的时窗宽度。Offset audio and video in time, and measure the width of the window within which they still fuse into one event.

功能四 · 注意定位（抗干扰与多任务）Module 4 · Attention and distractor cost

cost · dB

比较单说话人与双说话人条件下的阈值，两者之差反映抵抗无关语音干扰、把注意锁在目标上的代价。Compare thresholds with one versus two talkers; the difference is the cost of ignoring an irrelevant voice.

Scoring

从 dB / ms 到 0–100 分From dB / ms to a 0–100 score

阈值本身不直观，所以每个维度先映射到 0–100，再按权重合成综合指数 AVIS。缺失的维度不参与归一化，不会因为没测而被拉低。Thresholds are not intuitive, so each dimension maps to 0–100 and they are weighted into a composite (AVIS). Missing dimensions are excluded from normalisation rather than dragging the score down.
