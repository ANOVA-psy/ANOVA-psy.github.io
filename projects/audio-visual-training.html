视听语音整合训练系统Audio-Visual Integration Training System

把实验方案实现为可运行的浏览器工具。被试在不同噪声、口型清晰度和音画错位条件下判断语音，系统记录每一个试次，并把它折算成可比较的 dB、ms 和 0–100 分。 An experiment protocol turned into a working browser tool. Listeners judge speech under varying noise, mouth clarity and audio-visual offset; every trial is recorded and reduced to comparable dB, ms and a 0–100 score.

项目类型：课题组项目｜本人负责：前端实现｜2026｜Web 实验平台 Type: lab project · My part: front-end implementation · 2026 · web experiment

问题The problem

日常交流里我们一直在做多感官整合：嘈杂环境下盯着对方口型会更容易听懂，声音和画面差了几十毫秒，理解就会变吃力。这些能力在听损、康复训练和老年群体中差异很大，但「听得清不清楚」不是一个可以拿来比较的结论。 Everyday listening is multisensory: watching a speaker's mouth helps in noise, and a few tens of milliseconds of audio-visual asynchrony makes comprehension harder. These abilities vary widely across listeners — but "can you hear it clearly" is not a comparable finding.

这个平台要解决的是把它测出来：四种能力拆成四个可独立施测的任务，每个任务用心理物理阶梯逼近阈值，再统一到 0–100 分，让训练前后的变化可以被比较。 This platform makes it measurable: four separable abilities become four tasks, each converging on a threshold through a staircase, all reported on one 0–100 scale so change over training can be compared.

使用者是两拨人：被试要一眼看懂现在该做什么，不能靠主试在旁边念；实验者要能改参数、回看历史、按被试导出数据。这两个需求决定了页面结构和数据链路。 Two kinds of users: participants, who need to know what to do without being told twice, and experimenters, who need to change parameters, review history and export per-subject data. Those two needs shaped the screens and the data chain.

我负责的部分What I built

实验设计、测量指标与刺激材料由课题组确定；我负责把它们实现成能跑起来的系统，下面三块是我从头写的。The lab set the design, the measures and the stimuli. I built them into a working system — the three parts below are mine.

页面与流程Screens and flow

被试端入口、四类任务作答界面、结果页与历史记录页。什么状态下能进下一阶段、什么时候该锁定重测，都由前端状态机控制，避免被试中途刷新把流程走乱。The entry screen, the four task screens, the report and the history view — with a front-end state machine deciding what may follow what, so a refresh mid-session cannot scramble the procedure.

音画调度与阶梯控制AV timing and staircases

用 Web Audio 把声音与画面的间隔控到毫秒，按任务分别实现 2-down/1-up、3-down/1-up 与时窗外推，答对答错推着难度升降，接近上限时换一个维度继续。Web Audio for millisecond audio-visual offsets; per-task staircases (2-down/1-up, 3-down/1-up, temporal-window pushing) that move difficulty with each response and switch dimension near the ceiling.

数据链路Data chain

试次级记录 → 曲线拟合 → 阈值 → 维度分 → 综合指数，一条链路算下来；按被试导出 CSV / JSON，直接进统计软件，不需要人工抄一遍。Trial records → curve fit → threshold → dimension score → composite in one chain, with per-subject CSV/JSON export that goes straight into statistics software.

三个关键设计Three design decisions

这三条决定了数据能不能拿来比较，也是实现时最费劲的地方。These three decide whether the numbers are comparable at all — and they were the hard part to build.

01

前后测同构，差异才可比Identical pre- and post-test

同一个被试在同一次会话里走完前测、训练、后测，前后测用同一套刺激与同一套拟合方法。差异只来自训练，不来自换了题目。One participant runs pre-test, training and post-test in a single session; pre- and post-test share stimuli and fitting method, so any difference comes from training, not from new items.

02

每个任务一套阶梯规则A staircase per task

四个任务的难度维度不同（SNR、模糊度、时窗、干扰源），所以不能用一套通用阶梯。每条规则单独实现、单独验证收敛行为。The four tasks vary along different dimensions — SNR, blur, temporal window, distractor — so one generic staircase would not do. Each rule was implemented and checked for convergence on its own.

03

先定判准，再定义分数Criterion first, score second

阈值本身不直观，所以先把每个维度的阈值映射到 0–100 并写清锚点，再按权重合成。缺失维度不参与归一化，不会因为没测而被拉低。Thresholds are not intuitive, so each dimension maps to 0–100 with written anchors before weighting. Missing dimensions are excluded from normalisation rather than dragging the score down.

技术细节Technical detail

同一批内容也可以直接看源码：四个模块、三阶段、一套阶梯控制器、一条评分链路。All of this is in the repository: four modules, three phases, one staircase controller, one scoring chain.

threshold · dB

功能一 · 噪音抗干扰Module 1 · Speech in noise

在不同信噪比下识别语音，拟合出正确率达到判准时的 SNR 阈值；越低说明越能在噪声里听清。Identify speech at varying SNR; the fitted threshold is the SNR at criterion — lower is better.

gain · dB

功能二 · 视觉代偿Module 2 · Visual compensation

固定听觉条件，比较有清晰口型与口型模糊两种条件下的阈值差，差值即视觉增益。Holding audio fixed, compare thresholds with clear versus blurred mouth cues; the difference is visual gain.

window · ms

功能三 · 异步校准Module 3 · Temporal recalibration

让声音与画面在时间轴上错开，测出仍能整合成单一事件的时窗宽度。Offset audio and video, and measure the window within which they still fuse into one event.

cost · dB

功能四 · 注意定位Module 4 · Attention

比较单说话人与双说话人条件下的阈值，差值反映抵抗无关语音干扰的代价。Compare thresholds with one versus two talkers; the difference is the cost of ignoring an irrelevant voice.

三阶段流程Three-phase procedure

  • 01 PRE
    前测 · 建立基线Pre-test · baseline
    按预设顺序遍历各档 SNR / 模糊度 / SOA，逐试次记录正误，拟合心理物理曲线得到基线阈值。Walk preset SNR, blur and SOA levels; fit a psychometric curve per module.
  • 02 TRAIN
    训练 · 自适应难度Training · adaptive
    读取前测拟合结果推荐起始难度，再由阶梯控制器按作答动态升降，跑满试次或反转次数后结束。Starting difficulty derived from pre-test fits, then moved by response until trials or reversals run out.
  • 03 POST
    后测 · 量化变化Post-test · change
    重复前测设计，输出同一套阈值与分数，与前测对比得到训练效应；历史页可跨会话看趋势。Repeat the design, output the same thresholds, compare with baseline; trends across sessions live on the history page.

阶梯规则与起点推导Staircase rules and starting points

功能一用 2-down/1-up 调 SNR，以「前测阈值 + 2 dB」作为首个 SNR，使开局正确率接近九成，避免一上来就挫败；功能二用 3-down/1-up 推进模糊度档位，起始档位按视觉增益决定；功能三按视觉领先与听觉领先两侧分别外推和内缩时窗边界；功能四先在主轴上调 SNR，触及安全上限后改调干扰源衰减。所有推导值都会被夹到预设合理区间内。 Module 1 uses 2-down/1-up on SNR and starts at "baseline threshold + 2 dB" so the first trials are mostly correct; module 2 pushes blur levels with 3-down/1-up, starting from visual gain; module 3 pushes and pulls each side of the temporal window; module 4 adjusts SNR first and switches to distractor attenuation once it hits the ceiling. Every derived value is clamped to a sane range.
