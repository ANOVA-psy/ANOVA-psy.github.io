中学教师教学评价 Agent 原型Teaching Evaluation Agent Prototype

让评价结论能回到具体课堂证据。老师上传一节课的录音和教案，得到一份每条结论都挂着课堂实录时间点的评课报告。 Making every judgement point back to classroom evidence. A teacher uploads a lesson recording and plan, and gets a review report where each conclusion carries a timestamp from the transcript.

项目类型：课题组项目｜本人负责：任务拆解、评价规则、界面原型｜2026 Type: lab project · My part: task decomposition, rubric, wireframes · 2026

这是设计阶段的原型，不是已上线的在线系统。真实系统与业务数据不便公开，本页展示的是任务拆解、评价规则与脱敏线框；线框表示页面结构和功能入口，不代表这些功能都已经实现。 This is a design-stage prototype, not a live system. The real system and its data are not public; what follows is the decomposition, the rubric and sanitised wireframes. Wireframes show structure and entry points, not features that are already built.

问题The problem

评课这件事，难的不是给分，是让分有出处。一节 40 分钟的课，教研员能记住的片段很少，最后写出来的评价往往是「师生互动较好」「目标基本达成」这类话——老师看完不知道该改哪一步。 The hard part of lesson review is not scoring, it is making the score traceable. A reviewer remembers very little of a 40-minute lesson, so reports end up saying "interaction was good" or "objectives were broadly met" — and the teacher still does not know what to change.

课题组要做的，是把这件事交给模型：给它教学设计和课堂转写，让它按维度打分，并且每个分数都要能指回实录里的具体时间点。使用者有三种：上课的老师、做研究的教研人员、维护系统的管理人员。 The lab's goal was to hand this to a model: give it the lesson plan and the transcript, have it score by dimension, and require every score to point at a timestamp. Three groups use it: teachers, researchers, and the administrators who keep it running.

我负责的部分What I owned

项目由课题组共同完成。我负责把「评课」翻译成模型能执行的任务，定下评价规则，并把界面画出来。A team effort. My part was translating "lesson review" into a task a model can run, writing the rules, and drawing the interface.

任务拆解Task decomposition

把「评一节课」拆成材料解析、维度评分、证据定位、建议生成四步，每一步都有明确的输入与输出，单独拿出来也能验对错。Broke "evaluate a lesson" into parsing, scoring, evidence and suggestions — each with defined inputs and outputs, each checkable on its own.

评价维度Rubric

参与设计目标清晰度、任务设计、师生互动、评价一致性四个维度，并为每个维度写下「什么算好」的判定描述。Co-designed four dimensions — clarity of objectives, task design, teacher–student interaction, alignment of assessment — with written criteria for what counts as good in each.

输出规则Output rules

三层结构：评什么、什么算好、怎么呈现。字段固定下来，同一节课跑两次才不会给出两套说法。Three layers: what is judged, what counts as good, how it is presented. Fixed fields are what keep two runs from telling two stories.

界面线框Wireframes

按三种角色画出七个界面：工作台、课例列表、课例详情、公共资源、评课报告、教研助手等，并标注了每个页面的状态与入口。Seven screens across three roles — workspace, lesson list, lesson detail, shared resources, review report, assistant — with states and entry points marked for each.

设计思路：三种人，各看各的Who sees what

这不是三套系统。同一套课例库、同一套公共资源，三种身份进来看到的是不同的工作台。Not three systems. One lesson library, one shared pool of resources — three identities walk into different workbenches.

给上课的老师For the teacher

进门先看到「今天要做什么」：上传 / 新建 / 批量导入三个动作，最近五节课，哪节课的报告出来了。个人空间按状态管课例，公共空间按教研组、学校、区域分层找参考。They land on "what to do today": three actions, the last five lessons, which reports are ready. Personal space tracks lessons by state; shared space is layered by group, school and region.

给做研究的教研人员For the researcher

功能权限和老师一致，只是首页换成研究主题与可引用的资源，个人空间按主题和标签组织而不是按状态。另有两个共享模块：自定义编码体系，以及数据使用权限的申请与审计。Identical permissions; the home page switches to research topics and citable resources, and the personal space organises by topic rather than state. Two extra modules: custom coding schemes, and data-access requests with an audit trail.

给教研管理人员For the administrator

看运行面板与流水线状态、发版本与灰度、管资源与用户权限、查日志与审计。这些入口对前两种身份不显示——不是藏起来，是根本不该出现在他们的导航里。Runtime and pipeline status, releases and staged rollouts, resources, users and permissions, logs and audit. None of it appears in the other two roles' navigation — not hidden, simply not theirs.

一条贯穿的约束：One constraint runs through it: 三种身份共用一张课例表，差别只在「看什么、先看什么、能改什么」。所以权限是渲染层的差别，不是三份数据——否则评课报告、研究分析和运营统计会各说各话。 All three share one lesson table; what differs is what they see, what comes first, and what they may change. Permissions are a rendering-layer difference, not three copies of the data — otherwise reports, analyses and statistics would each tell a different story.

设计上的三个决定Three design decisions

都围绕同一件事：让老师愿意用、看得懂、改得动。All three serve one goal: a teacher should want to use it, understand it, and be able to act on it.

01

用老师的话重命名Speak the teacher's language

语料改叫课例，转录文本改叫课堂实录，编码分析改叫课堂评价，分析报告改叫评课报告。名字一换，老师才知道这是自己的东西。Corpus became lesson, transcript became class record, coding became evaluation, report became review report. Renaming is what makes it feel like theirs.

02

首页是工作台，不是数据大屏A workbench, not a dashboard

只留三个动作和最近五节课，统计图表和版本记录撤掉。老师打开系统是为了处理下一节课，不是为了看总量趋势。Three actions and the last five lessons; charts and changelogs removed. A teacher opens the system to handle the next lesson, not to study aggregate trends.

03

报告按评课现场组织A report made for the review meeting

第一页给总体评价、亮点与待改进项；第二页每条结论挂一段课堂实录，教师可以批注、可以改分；第三页数据附录可选。导出 PDF 或 Word，直接带进教研组。Page one: verdict, highlights, what to fix. Page two: each conclusion pinned to a transcript excerpt, annotatable and re-scorable. Page three: optional appendix. Export to PDF or Word.

一节课走完这条链路One lesson through the pipeline

拆任务时用的就是这张图，它同时对应线框里的页面顺序。This is the diagram used to scope the work; it also matches the page order in the wireframes.

  • 01
    提交材料Submit
    教学设计、课堂实录、作业或反思，单节提交或批量导入。Lesson plan, recording, assignments or reflection — single or batch.
  • 02
    结构化解析Parse
    把非结构化材料切成可定位的片段，作为后面判断的证据来源。Split materials into locatable segments later judgements can cite.
  • 03
    多维评价Evaluate
    按预设维度与判定规则逐项打分，没给出对应证据的不算完成。Score each dimension against written rules; a score without evidence does not count as done.
  • 04
    生成报告Report
    组织成教师能读的报告，而不是一堆术语和总分。Assemble scores and evidence into something a teacher can read.
  • 05
    改进与沉淀Improve
    输出可执行的改课建议，课例归入教研主题供后续比较。Output actionable changes and file the lesson under a research theme.
