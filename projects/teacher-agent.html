中学教师教学评估与改进智能体Teaching Evaluation & Improvement Agent

围绕教师日常工作设计产品流程，从上传课堂录音到生成结构化评课报告，串联材料提交、教学分析与结果呈现。 Designed a teacher-centered workflow, connecting lesson recording uploads with instructional analysis and structured evaluation reports.

Design Logic

设计思路：三种人，各看各的Who sees what

这不是三套系统。同一套课例库、同一套公共资源，三种身份进来看到的是不同的工作台。Not three systems. One lesson library, one shared pool of resources — three identities walk into different workbenches.

给上课的老师For the teacher

上传 → 看报告 → 改下一节upload → read → revise

进门先看到"今天要做什么"：三个动作（上传 / 新建 / 批量导入）、最近五节课、哪节课的报告出来了。个人空间按状态管课例；公共空间按教研组、学校、区域分层，同教研组的老师可以在校内或校外顺畅地讨论、转发和引用课例。They land on "what to do today": three actions, the last five lessons, and which reports are ready. Personal space tracks lessons by state; shared space is layered by group, school and region, where teachers in the same group can discuss, forward and cite lessons whether they are on or off campus.

给做研究的教研人员For the researcher

同一套功能，换一种组织same tools, different layout

功能权限和老师完全一致，只是首页换成研究主题与可引用的资源，个人空间按主题和标签组织而不是按状态。另有两个共享模块：自定义编码体系，以及数据使用权限的申请与审计。Identical permissions; the home page switches to research topics and citable resources, and the personal space organises by topic rather than by state. Two extra modules: custom coding schemes, and data-access requests with an audit trail.

给管理人员For the administrator

数据大屏 · 直观审计a dashboard · direct auditing

看的是一块数据大屏：区域、学校、学科三个层级的课例数量、评课覆盖率、四个维度的分布与集中出现的薄弱环节，一眼看得出"哪些学校在用、用得怎么样、问题集中在哪"。可以一直下钻到某一所学校、某个学科、某节课的原始报告。它的用途是给管理人员的直观审计与督导决策——不必读完几十份文字报告就能判断现状。What they get is a dashboard: lesson counts, review coverage, the distribution of the four dimensions and the weaknesses that keep recurring, broken down by region, school and subject — who is using it, how well, and where the problems cluster. It drills all the way down to one school, one subject, one lesson's original report. It exists so that administrators can audit at a glance: no need to read dozens of narrative reports before judging the state of things.

My Contribution

我负责的部分What I owned

这个项目由课题组共同完成，我主要负责把"评课"这件事翻译成模型能执行的任务：拆步骤、定评价规则、写 Skill、画界面。A team effort; my part was translating "lesson review" into a task a model can run — the steps, the scoring rules, the Skill, and the interface.

任务拆解Task decomposition

一句需求 → 四个环节one ask → four steps

把"评一节课"拆成材料解析、维度打分、证据定位、建议生成，每一步都有明确的输入输出，单独拿出来也能验。Broke "evaluate a lesson" into parsing, scoring, evidence and suggestions — each with defined inputs and a way to check it alone.

评价维度Rubric

4 个维度4 dimensions

目标清晰度、任务设计、师生互动、评价一致性。每个维度都要求指向实录里的具体时间点，不给没有出处的分。Clarity of objectives, task design, teacher–student interaction, alignment of assessment — each one must point at a timestamp.

界面线框Wireframes

7 屏 · 三种角色7 screens · 3 roles

教师、教研人员、管理员共用一套数据，界面按角色渲染：教师看"我的课和待办"，研究者看"研究主题和可引用资源"。Teachers, researchers and admins share one dataset; the interface renders by role — my lessons for teachers, topics and citable resources for researchers.

Skill 封装Skill packaging

把评课做成可复用的能力review as a reusable capability

除了任务拆解和提示词，我还把整套评课能力写成了一个 Skill：什么时候触发、需要哪些材料、按什么规则打分、结果长什么样，全部固定在同一份定义里。好处是换一所学校、换一套教研标准时，改的是配置和规则，不用把任务重新拆一遍；同一节课被不同角色调用，也不至于各说各话。Beyond the decomposition and prompts, I packaged the whole review capability into a Skill: when it triggers, which materials it needs, how it scores, and what the output looks like — all pinned in one definition. The point is reuse: a different school or a different set of review standards means editing configuration and rules, not re-deriving the task; and the same lesson called by different roles still comes back with one story.

Flow

从一节课，到区域的一张图From one lesson to one picture of a region

这条链路把三种角色串起来：老师上传、模型评课、同行在公共空间交流与比较、助手接着做个性化改进，最后汇总成管理者一眼能看懂的审计视图。顺序对应线框里的页面。This chain ties the three roles together: a teacher uploads, the model reviews, colleagues discuss and compare in the shared space, the assistant carries on with individual improvement, and it all rolls up into an audit view a manager can read at a glance. The order matches the wireframe screens.

  • 01 · 提交与批量导入Submit and import
    材料先进系统，一节课可以是一堆文件Materials come in; a lesson can be many files
    教学设计、课堂实录（录音或视频）、作业与反思，可以单节提交，也可以整批导入。文件按名字自动分组，一个文件夹就是一节课；学科、年级、教材批量填一次，剩下的等它跑完。Lesson plans, recordings, assignments and reflections arrive one at a time or in bulk. Files group themselves by name so a folder becomes a lesson, and subject, grade and textbook are set once for the whole batch.
  • 02 · 结构化解析Parse
    把一节课变成能引用的证据Turn a lesson into citable evidence
    录音转写并对齐时间轴，教案、实录、反思被切成带时间码的片段。后面每一条评分和建议都要指回这里的具体位置——没有这一段，评课就还是凭印象说话。Audio is transcribed and aligned to the timeline; plans, records and reflections are cut into timestamped segments. Every score and suggestion downstream points back to one of them — without this step, review is still an impression.
  • 03 · 四维评价Evaluate
    打分必须带出处Every score carries a source
    目标清晰度、任务设计、师生互动、评价一致性逐项评定，每一项都要引用实录里的片段。写不出出处的分数不进报告，避免"感觉这课不错"混进结论里。Objectives, task design, interaction and assessment alignment are judged one by one, each citing an excerpt. A score that cannot cite its source never reaches the report, so "it felt like a good lesson" does not slip into the findings.
  • 04 · 生成评课报告Report
    一页结论，一页证据One page of verdict, one of evidence
    第一页是总体评价、课堂亮点、待改进项和关键指标；第二页每条结论挂着原文片段，教师可以批注、也可以改分；数据附录可选。导出 PDF 或 Word，直接带进评课会。Page one holds the verdict, highlights, what to fix and the key indicators; page two pins each conclusion to its excerpt, annotatable and re-scorable; the appendix is optional. Export to PDF or Word and take it into the review meeting.
  • 05 · 课后同行交流Peer discussion
    讨论落在具体的时间点上Discussion anchored to timestamps
    报告进入教研组的公共空间，同组老师就着某个时间点批注、补充自己的观察、提出不同判断，也可以把某一段直接转给带教师傅。讨论留痕，下一轮评课时看得到上一轮的争议点。The report lands in the group's shared space, where colleagues annotate at the exact timestamp, add their own observations or disagree, and forward a segment to a mentor. The thread stays with the lesson, so the next round can see what was contested.
  • 06 · 公共空间同课异构Same topic, different takes
    同一课题的不同上法，并排看得见Variants of one topic, side by side
    同一课题、不同老师、不同班级的课例放在一起比较：同一维度的分差、各自的证据片段、改课前后留下的轨迹。校内校外的老师都能看、能引用，也能把自己的版本传上来，慢慢攒成一个可参考的变式库。Lessons on the same topic by different teachers and classes sit side by side: score gaps on the same dimension, each one's evidence, and the trail from revision to revision. Teachers on or off campus can read, cite and add their own version, so the pool slowly becomes a library of variants.
  • 07 · 教学助手 AgentTeaching assistant agent
    改进建议是给这个人的Advice meant for this teacher
    带着自己的报告和历史课例继续追问：只要针对"提问链怎么设计"的建议，或者按自己的教学习惯重写教案、逐字稿和课件。建议会引用自己过去课例里的片段，而不是通用套话。The teacher keeps asking, with their own reports and past lessons in hand: advice aimed only at how the questioning chain is built, or a rewritten plan, script and slides in their own style. Suggestions cite their own earlier lessons rather than generic advice.
  • 08 · 区域数据大屏Regional dashboard
    给管理者的直观审计Auditing at a glance
    课例量、评课覆盖率、四个维度的分布与反复出现的薄弱环节，按区域、学校、学科三层汇总，可以一直下钻到某一节课的原始报告。管理人员用它看整体进展、安排督导，不必先读完几十份文字报告。Lesson volume, review coverage, the distribution across four dimensions and the weaknesses that keep recurring — rolled up by region, school and subject, drillable down to a single lesson's report. Administrators use it to see progress and plan supervision without first reading dozens of narrative reports.
