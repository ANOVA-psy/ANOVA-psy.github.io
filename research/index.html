研究与论文Research & publications

都是从「人怎么理解别人」这一个问题问出来的：一条走向大模型的能力边界，一条走向多感觉整合，一条走向数字时代的真实行为。下面五篇的链接都逐条点过。 All of it grows from one question — how people understand each other. One thread leads to the limits of LLMs, one to multisensory integration, one to real behaviour online. Every link below was opened and checked.

Directions

四条研究线索Four threads

大模型的社会认知与评测Social cognition in LLMs

ToM · 隐性偏见 · 评测框架

模型能不能推断他人的信念与意图？失败时是能力不足还是答非所问？参与 MIST 框架的维度设计与实验，用间接任务绕开模型的回避。Can a model infer beliefs and intentions — and when it fails, is that capability or evasion? Contributed to MIST's dimension design and experiments, using indirect tasks to bypass refusal.

多感觉整合与康复训练Multisensory integration

视听语音 · 时窗 · 阈值

声音与画面如何合成一个事件、噪声下口型能补偿多少——并把这些问题做成可施测的实验平台（见"项目"）。How audio and vision fuse into one event, and how much speechreading compensates in noise — operationalised as a testable platform (see Projects).

EEG 与认知神经机制EEG & cognitive neuroscience

功能连接 · 有效连接 · 学习困难

用 EEG 功能与有效连接刻画阅读与数学任务中的脑网络差异，关注儿童学习困难的神经标记。Characterising brain-network differences during reading and arithmetic with functional and effective connectivity, with a focus on children with learning difficulties.

用户行为与社会参与User behaviour & participation

问卷 · 访谈 · 动机分层

研究年轻一代如何划分公私领域、数字媒介动机如何影响社会参与，并把这些结论转化为可用的用户洞察。How young adults draw public/private boundaries online, and how digital motives shape participation — translated into usable user insights.

    MIST: Towards Multi-dimensional Implicit BiaS Evaluation of LLMs for Theory of Mind

    Li, Y., Liu, H., Liu, H., Wang, K., Wei, Y., & Hu, Y.

    arXiv 预印本 · arXiv:2506.14161 · 最新版 v3（2026-01）arXiv preprint · arXiv:2506.14161 · latest v3 (Jan 2026)

    把刻板印象的内容模型重组为能力、社交性、道德三个维度的 ToM 失败，用词汇联想与情感归因两个间接任务测量，在 8 个模型上做了实验。 Reconceives stereotype content as multidimensional ToM failure across competence, sociability and morality, measured with two indirect tasks (word association, affective attribution) across eight models.
