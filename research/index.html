    MIST: Towards Multi-dimensional Implicit BiaS Evaluation of LLMs for Theory of Mind

    Li, Y., Liu, H., Liu, H., Wang, K., Wei, Y., & Hu, Y.

    CogSci 2026 · 已发表 · arXiv:2506.14161CogSci 2026 · published · arXiv:2506.14161

    把刻板印象的内容模型重组为能力、社交性、道德三个维度的 ToM 失败，用词汇联想与情感归因两个间接任务测量，在 8 个模型上做了实验。 Reconceives stereotype content as multidimensional ToM failure across competence, sociability and morality, measured with two indirect tasks (word association, affective attribution) across eight models.
