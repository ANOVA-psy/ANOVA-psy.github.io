研究与论文Research and publications

目前的研究集中在大模型的心智理论评测：把「理解他人」拆成可测量的任务，比较不同模型在哪一类情境上失败，再追问这些失败对应什么样的任务条件或训练方式。下面的清单按年份倒序，每条写明发表信息、作者位次与我的具体工作。 My current work centres on measuring theory of mind in large language models: turning "understanding others" into measurable tasks, comparing where different models fail, and asking what those failures say about task conditions or training. The list below runs newest first, with venue, author position and what I actually did.

  • 2026

    MIST: Towards Multi-dimensional Implicit BiaS Evaluation of LLMs for Theory of Mind

    Li, Y., Liu, H., Liu, H., Wang, K., Wei, Y., & Hu, Y.

    CogSci 2026 · 已发表 · arXiv:2506.14161CogSci 2026 · published · arXiv:2506.14161

    把刻板印象的内容模型重组为能力、社交性、道德三个维度上的心智理论失败，用词汇联想与情感归因两个间接任务测量，在 8 个模型上完成比较。我负责实验设计、数据分析与结果绘图。 Reconceives stereotype content as theory-of-mind failure across competence, sociability and morality, measured with two indirect tasks (word association, affective attribution) across eight models. I did the experiment design, the analysis and the figures.
